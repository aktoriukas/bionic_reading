# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Bionic Reading processes text from webpages locally in your browser to apply
visual emphasis. Webpage content is not stored, sent to the developer, or shared
with third parties.

The extension stores one setting—whether Bionic Reading should remain enabled
across pages—using Chrome's synced extension storage. Chrome may sync this
preference through your Google account; the developer cannot access it.

Bionic Reading has no servers, analytics, advertising, or remote code.

Questions can be submitted through the project's
[GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/aktoriukas/bionic_reading/issues).
